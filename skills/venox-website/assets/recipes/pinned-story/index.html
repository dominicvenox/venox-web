A fictional collection exploring three practical questions about how objects come apart, stay useful, and begin again.

01 / Keep

Begin with
what remains.

Before choosing a new material, examine the object you already have. A durable frame may need a replaceable surface, not a replacement life.

  • Keep sound structural parts.
  • Make wear surfaces replaceable.
  • Write down how to repair them.

02 / Separate

Make the next
step legible.

A beautiful assembly should have an understandable reverse. Accessible fasteners and identified materials help the next person take it apart.

  • Prefer accessible connections.
  • Identify materials where they meet.
  • Explain the disassembly order.

03 / Return

Design an
actual return.

A loop is a plan, not a symbol. Find the local route that can accept the material and describe its requirements accurately.

  • Name a verified destination.
  • State its preparation requirements.
  • Keep the guidance up to date.
